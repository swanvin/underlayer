# UNDERLAYER INTERNAL STANDARD v1.0  
Swan Labs Internal Measurement Constitution

---

## I. Purpose

Underlayer exists to map universal structural laws that govern system behavior across capital, time, flow, entropy, feedback, and alignment.

It is an internal instrumentation framework.

It does not execute.  
It does not transact.  
It does not advise.  

It measures.

---

## II. Architectural Layers

L0 — Law  
Immutable universal principle.

L1 — Measurement Surface  
Defines variables that represent the law in a given system.

L2 — Module (SPEC)  
Formalized instrument mapping one law to measurable variables.

L3 — Tool (LIVE)  
Executable interface surfacing measurements.

L4 — Execution Layer  
Intervention, automation, or capital deployment.

Underlayer governs L0–L2.  
Products may extend into L3–L4.

---

## III. Module Schema (Mandatory)

Every module must define:

1. Module ID
2. Name
3. Governing Law (L0)
4. Measurement Surface (L1)
5. Inputs
6. Outputs
7. Failure Mode
8. Interaction Surface
9. Defensive / Offensive Orientation

Incomplete modules do not ship.

---

## IV. Product Mapping Gate

Before any product build:

1. Which law governs it?
2. Which module(s) activate?
3. Defensive or offensive?
4. L2, L3, or L4?
5. What measurable variable proves success?
6. What failure mode is prevented?

If unclear, build pauses.

---

## V. Interaction Doctrine

Modules influence each other:

- Entropy degrades Flow
- Flow amplifies Compound
- Loop reinforces trajectory
- Align determines efficiency
- Ignite shifts system state

Products must account for cross-module effects.

---

## VI. Stabilize Before Optimize Rule

1. Detect entropy
2. Stabilize structure
3. Optimize flow
4. Amplify compounding
5. Reinforce positive loops
6. Maintain alignment

Skipping stabilization introduces fragility.

---

## VII. Graduation Rule

A module becomes an entity when:

- Sustained revenue exists
- Regulatory/liability exposure emerges
- Dedicated governance is required
- Market identity is distinct

Otherwise it remains internal.

---

## VIII. Internal Advantage Clause

Underlayer doctrine remains proprietary.

Public products may reference measurement and optimization,
but not expose law taxonomy, interaction grid, or internal scoring logic.

Doctrine compounds as intellectual capital.
